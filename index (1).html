Please read before booking

Booking, payment & safety conditions

We discuss the fare and any additional charges before confirming the booking.

🧾 Booking confirmation

  • Submitting a request does not confirm a taxi.
  • Availability, route, timing and final fare must be confirmed by our team.
  • Keep the written confirmation for your records.

💳 Payment schedule

  • 20% advance at booking, once terms are agreed.
  • 60% at pickup.
  • Remaining 20% before drop.
  • Obtain payment acknowledgement or receipt.

🔎 Extras & changes

  • Discuss late-night, food, parking, tolls, taxes, extra travel and other extras before booking.
  • Agree cancellation, advance and refund terms before payment.
  • Changes to route or timing may affect the fare.

No online payment is taken on this website. Never share a UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or banking password. Use seat belts and share accurate trip details.
