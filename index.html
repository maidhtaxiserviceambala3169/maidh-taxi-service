Fare, extra charges & payment terms

Discuss and confirm the complete trip estimate and terms with Maidh Taxi Service before the booking is finalised.

Charges to discuss before booking

Please ask us to explain which charges apply to your specific trip. Depending on the route and arrangement, these may include:

  • Late-night or early-morning charges, if applicable
  • Food or driver allowance, if applicable
  • Parking fees
  • Tolls, state taxes and other route-related taxes
  • Extra kilometres, waiting time, route changes or additional travel
No surprises: Any applicable extra charges should be discussed with you and agreed before the booking is confirmed. Ask for the final fare breakdown in writing on WhatsApp.

Proposed payment schedule

Payment schedule to be confirmed with the booking team before you pay:

20%At booking confirmation
60%At pickup time
20%Before drop-off
The percentages are based on the agreed trip amount. Confirm the total fare, payment method, cancellation/refund terms and any exceptions with us before making a payment. Do not pay until you have received booking confirmation.
