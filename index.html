Please read before booking

Booking, payment & cancellation conditions

Clear expectations help keep every journey smooth.

🧾 Booking confirmation

  • Submitting the form sends a booking request, not a confirmed reservation.
  • Our team must confirm vehicle availability, pickup time, trip details and final fare.
  • Keep the WhatsApp confirmation for your records.
  • Driver and vehicle details should be shared before pickup where possible.

💳 Payment & receipt

  • No payment is processed by this website.
  • Confirm the fare, included kilometres, tolls, parking, state tax and driver allowance before paying.
  • Pay only through a method agreed with the service.
  • Ask for a receipt or written payment acknowledgement after payment.

🔄 Changes & cancellation

  • Tell us as early as possible if your time, route or passenger count changes.
  • Any advance, cancellation charge or refund terms must be agreed before payment.
  • Delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures or flight/train changes may affect pickup time.
  • Do not assume a booking is cancelled until you receive acknowledgement.
Safety and conduct: Please use seat belts, do not request unlawful or unsafe travel, and share accurate pickup details. The service may decline a request if a suitable vehicle or legally required documents are unavailable. These conditions are a practical summary and should be reviewed for compliance with applicable law before commercial use.
